The Bridge at Calvary

Prayer Wall

Anyone can post a request, and the person asking chooses who sees it: the whole church, or the pastors only — lead pastor included. Church-wide requests appear on the wall for everyone; pastors-only requests never leave the staff, though the member always sees their own.

Pastors see every request. The lead pastor also gets admin. Edward owns the account, and the owner section exists only when he's logged in — no one else sees it, not even locked.

How the app knows who you are: the phone number is the account key. Sign in and it's looked up in the church directory, and your role comes back with it — member, pastor, lead pastor, or owner. Nobody picks their own role; the office sets it, and only Edward can set a lead pastor. Tap a demo account on the sign-in screen to fill a number, or type one that isn't on file to see the rejection.

Members go straight in. Pastors, the lead pastor, and Edward then enter a PIN, since those accounts read private requests. PINs: pastors and Daniel 2026, Pastor Rachel 2210, Edward 00599.

Switch roles below and the app changes. Worth trying: as a pastor, tap I prayed on Miss Ruth's surgery request, then switch to Member and open Notices.
